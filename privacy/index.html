Privacy Policy

PlanWellPrintShop Assistant
Last updated: November 2026

PlanWellPrintShop Assistant is a private tool used only by the owner of the PlanWellPrintShop Etsy shop to manage that shop and its own Pinterest business account. It is not offered to the public.

What information the app uses

  • Information about the owner's own Pinterest business account, boards and Pins, used to create Pins for the shop's products and read their performance.
  • Information about the owner's own Etsy shop, such as listings, prices and sales totals, used to manage the shop and create a daily summary for the owner.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or sell personal information about other Pinterest or Etsy users.
  • It does not share any data with advertisers or other third parties.
  • It does not access any account other than the owner's own.

How information is stored

Access tokens and account information are stored privately and securely on the owner's own server, are used only to operate the app, and can be revoked by the owner at any time from their Pinterest or Etsy account settings.
